Privacy Policy — Peg Solitaire Master: Senku Zen

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Peg Solitaire Master: Senku Zen ("the app") handles information. The app is developed and published by Jack Hawk (a publisher pseudonym on Google Play). Contact: jackhawkdev@gmail.com.

Data the app handles

Local game data

Your board progress, move history, settings, and lifetime statistics (games played/won, best rank, best time, current streak) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you enable cloud save (below).

Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

The app can optionally connect to Google Play Games Services. Signing in is never required to play — every feature works fully offline and without an account. If you choose to connect:

  • Google Play Games provides the app with your Play Games player identity, used only to associate your cloud save with your account.
  • Your use of Google Play Games is also governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

Cloud save (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your game progress (board state and statistics) is synced to Google's cloud save storage tied to your Google account, so you can continue on another device. This data is stored by Google Play Games Services, not on the developer's own servers — the developer has no separate database and cannot access this data outside of what Google Play Games Services exposes.

Advertising

The app shows optional and periodic advertisements served by Google AdMob, used to grant in-game rewards (extra Undos, skipping a level) and, past a certain point in Levels Mode, shown periodically between levels. Playing the app's core game never requires watching an ad. Where required by law (for example, GDPR in the EU/UK/EEA, and similar regions), the app asks for your consent — via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form — before requesting any ad that uses personal data for personalization; if you decline or consent has not yet been resolved, the app still functions normally but ad-gated bonuses (extra Undos, Skip Level) are simply shown as unavailable rather than requesting an ad anyway. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and other information to select and measure ads, as described in Google's own Privacy Policy and AdMob's own data-use disclosures. This consent prompt is shown once, the first time it is required, when the app starts.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional, one-time purchases (premium Theme Store skins) processed entirely through Google Play Billing. The app itself never collects, sees, or stores your payment/card details — Google Play handles the entire transaction, and only tells the app whether a purchase succeeded and which item was bought. Purchases are non-consumable (bought once, yours permanently, recoverable at any time via the in-app "Restore Purchases" action) and are tied to your Google Play account, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Google Play's own Terms of Service.

Feedback ("Send Feedback" in Settings)

If you use the in-app "Send Feedback" form, the app opens your device's own email app with a pre-filled message addressed to jackhawkdev@gmail.com. Nothing is sent automatically — you choose whether to actually send the email from your own email app. Any information in that email (including your email address and whatever you write) is received directly by the developer's email inbox, the same as any other email you send.

What this app does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are present in this app.
  • The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, or call logs.
  • The developer does not sell or share your data with third parties, other than Google Play Games Services (required for the optional sign-in/cloud-save features above), Google AdMob (required for the advertising described above), Google Play Billing (required for the optional in-app purchases described above), and your own email app (only if you choose to send feedback).

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program.

Changes to this policy
