Account & Data Deletion — Peg Solitaire Master: Senku Zen

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Peg Solitaire Master: Senku Zen (developed by Jack Hawk) does not create or store any account of its own. The app only optionally connects to your existing Google Play Games account for sign-in and cloud save. This page explains how to delete that account connection and any associated data, and how to delete your locally saved data on-device.

1. Delete your Google Play Games account connection and cloud data

Because this app has no developer-run account system, the account and its associated data (your Play Games player identity and your cloud-saved game progress for this app) are held entirely by Google, not by the developer. To request deletion:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile picture/avatar → Data & privacy (or Manage data, depending on your app version).
  3. Find Peg Solitaire Master: Senku Zen in the list of games with stored data.
  4. Select Delete data / Clear data and confirm.

Alternatively, you can manage or delete this data directly from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy → Data from apps and services you use → find Play Games activity for this app.

Signing out from within the app's own Settings screen stops further syncing going forward, but does not by itself delete data already stored in Google's cloud save — use the steps above for that.

2. Delete your locally saved data (no Google account needed)

You can permanently delete all game data stored on your device, whether or not you ever signed in to Google Play Games:

  1. Open the app → Settings.
  2. Tap Reset Progress.
  3. Confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes, on your device: every saved game across all board layouts and levels, your level progress, and your lifetime statistics (games played/won, best rank, best time, streak).

What is deleted, what is retained, and for how long

  • Local, on-device data (saves, level progress, statistics): deleted immediately when you use Reset Progress in Settings. Nothing is retained anywhere else, since this data was never stored outside your device.
  • Cloud-synced data (your Play Games player identity, cloud-saved game progress): deleted immediately once you complete the Google-side steps in section 1 above. If you only use the in-app Reset Progress without also completing section 1, the previously synced copy in Google's cloud save may remain until it is next overwritten by a future sync — for guaranteed, immediate removal of cloud data, use section 1's steps.
  • Developer-held data: none. Peg Solitaire Master: Senku Zen has no developer-operated server or database of any kind — all account and cloud-save data is held entirely by Google Play Games Services, not by the developer, so there is nothing further for the developer to delete on their end.
